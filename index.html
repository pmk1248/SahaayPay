1. What Evidence To Save

  • 📷 Take Screenshots: Capture scam messages, fake websites, or profiles immediately.

3. What To Do Next

  • 📞 Call 1930 immediately to report suspected financial fraud.
  • 🌐 Visit cybercrime.gov.in to file an official report.
  • 🏦 Contact your bank to block your account or card.
